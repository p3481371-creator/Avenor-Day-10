Our story

A scent is the shortest road back to a moment.

AVENOR was born from a notebook of remembered scents: jasmine on a warm night, cedar after rain, amber on a grandmother's scarf. Seven years later, each bottle still begins there.

The process

From Field to Flacon

Eight to ten weeks per batch
  1. 01

    Sourcing

    Natural materials from twelve growers, bought in season.

  2. 02

    Blending

    Up to eighty notes per formula, weighed by hand.

  3. 03

    Maturing

    Six to eight weeks in cool, dark amber flasks.

  4. 04

    Bottling

    Filled, sealed and numbered, at most eight hundred at a time.
