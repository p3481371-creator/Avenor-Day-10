A Journey ofScent & Soul

AVENOR was born from a simple belief: fragrance has the power to connect us with our deepest emotions.

Our belief
A scent is the shortest road back to a moment. We bottle the moments worth returning to.

Every AVENOR fragrance starts with a memory, not a market brief. A summer evening, a walk through wet cedar, the warmth of skin after sun. Our perfumers work backwards from that feeling until the formula matches it.

We keep our batches small, our ingredients traceable and our bottles refillable, because a fragrance worth wearing every day should be made with the same patience it asks of you.

Not just a fragrance. A feeling.
Our journey

Seven Years
in the Making

From a notebook of remembered scents to four signatures worn around the world.

2019

A notebook of memories

AVENOR began as a notebook of scents remembered: jasmine on a warm night, cedar after rain, amber on a grandmother's scarf.

2021

Three hundred trials

Our first formula took more than three hundred trials before Amber Noir smelled exactly like the memory it was written from.

2023

Rooted in Grasse

We began sourcing jasmine directly from family growers around Grasse, picked by hand at dawn when the flowers are most fragrant.

2025

Four signatures

Blush Petal, Forest Whisper and Ocean Breeze joined Amber Noir, completing a wardrobe of four moods.

2026

The Discovery Set

All four fragrances in travel sprays, so everyone can find their signature before choosing a full bottle.
